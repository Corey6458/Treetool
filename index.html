This key is a screening tool for narrowing down field observations — it does not replace lab confirmation or a certified arborist's diagnosis. Oak wilt, Dutch elm disease, and boring insects are time-sensitive in Michigan: when in doubt, photograph the symptom, note the address, and escalate the same day rather than waiting for the next scheduled visit.
